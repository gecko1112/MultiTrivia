# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Estimation and trivia lovers: people who enjoy guessing numbers, words and places and like to compare how close they got. They play in short daily sessions, mostly on a phone or laptop, and usually arrive in one of two ways: a friend sent them the link, or they found the site on their own and have never heard of it. Both matter equally. Accounts are for people aged 16 and over.

## Product Purpose

Ballpark is a set of daily guessing and trivia games played in the browser. Its promise, in the owner's words: "estimation and trivia lovers, expand (and show off) your everyday knowledge". Success is a player who comes back each day, gets a little better at judging the world, and has friends to measure against.

## Positioning

Being roughly right wins. Most answers are estimates, scored by how close they land (often by order of magnitude), so a good guess beats not knowing. Everyone gets the same puzzles each day, and results are shared between friends only.

## Operating Context

- Five daily games: Survival (estimate numbers until your lives run out), More/Less, Guessless, Clueless and Wordy. A sixth section, the Atlas, has geography games: Flags, Outlines and Overland.
- Practice games, live party lobbies with a lobby code, optional drinking games (18+), levels and achievements.
- Friends are added by username or friend code. Daily results, leaderboards and profile pictures are visible to friends only.
- Runs as a plain Node.js server with static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework and no build step.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Playing is free. Ballpark Plus is an optional subscription: archive of past days, detailed statistics, streak saver, head-to-head record, extra question packs, unlimited practice and hosting party games. Free accounts get 3 practice games a day in each game and 3 party games to host as a trial; joining a lobby is always free. The numbers are a first proposal the owner has not confirmed.
- Prices shown on the site (€2.99 a month, €24.99 a year) are placeholders the owner has not confirmed. Plus is not on sale yet.
- Results are decided on the server, never in the browser. A demonstration on a public page may not be presented as a real, counted result.
- The site is in English. A German version is planned, not built.
- No tracking, no analytics, no third-party requests on normal pages. Fonts are served from the site itself.
- Undecided: the domain, the final prices, the operator's public contact details.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ballpark. Paid tier: Ballpark Plus.
- The game pages are the visual authority: the "By game" palette (each game has its own colour), Barlow Condensed for headings and Inter for text, light and dark mode. Public pages must look like the same product.
- Fermi, a small robot, lives on the game pages as a subtle easter egg. He never repeats a line.
- Voice: plain and brief. No marketing hype, no fake urgency, little text. Effects stay modest.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: the games themselves, 319 Survival questions with sources in their answer notes, the country data behind the Atlas, the pricing and legal pages.
- Absent, and not to be invented: player numbers, testimonials, press, ratings, awards.

## Product Principles

1. A guess is always worth making. Show how close, not just right or wrong.
2. Short by design. A day's games fit into a few minutes, and the words on screen stay few.
3. Fair between friends. Same puzzles for everyone, the server judges, nothing is revealed before you have played.
4. Free to play. Plus adds depth and never locks today's games.
5. Honest. No claim on the site that is not true today.
