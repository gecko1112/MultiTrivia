Refund policy

Last changed:

14 days, no questions

If you are not happy with Ballpark Plus, ask for a refund within 14 days of a payment and you get that payment back in full. This applies to the first payment and to every renewal. You do not have to give a reason. Plus ends when the refund is made.

How to ask

  • Write to with your Ballpark username, or
  • use the link in the receipt Paddle sent you by email, or go to paddle.net. Paddle is our reseller and makes the refund.

The money goes back to the payment method you used, usually within a few working days.

Your right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have a legal right to withdraw from the purchase within 14 days without giving a reason. The 14 days above cover this right; using it is as simple as sending the message described here.

Cancelling is not the same as a refund

You can cancel at any time in the Plus window on the site. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Plus stays until the end of the time you have paid for, and that time is not refunded unless you ask within the 14 days.

After 14 days

Later payments are normally not refunded. If something went wrong, for example you were charged twice or Plus did not work, write to us and we will sort it out.
