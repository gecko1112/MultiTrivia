Privacy policy

Last changed:

Ballpark stores as little about you as it can. There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics, and we do not sell or share data for marketing.

1. Who is responsible





2. What we store

  • Account: your username, your password in a form that cannot be read back (a salted scrypt hash), and the date the account was created. We do not ask for your real name or an email address.
  • Profile picture: if you choose to add one. It is shrunk to a small square in your browser before it is uploaded, and only your friends can see it. You can remove it at any time.
  • Friends: who your friends are, and open friend requests. A player you send a request to sees your username.
  • Game data: your results, statistics, achievements, and games in progress, so that they are there on every device and can be compared with other players.
  • Leaving the page during a game: for the daily games and party rounds we count how often the page was left while a game was running. This is a fair-play measure. Only administrators can see the count.
  • Party lobbies: the players, guesses and chat messages of a lobby exist only while the lobby is open. They are not saved.
  • Subscription: if you buy Plus: the plan, its start and end date, whether it renews, and the reference numbers Paddle gives us for the subscription and the customer. We never see or store card or bank details.
  • Technical data: like every website, the server receives your IP address in order to send you the pages. We also use it for a short time to slow down password guessing (ten minutes after wrong passwords) and to limit how many accounts can be created from one connection (one hour). It is not stored with your account.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the games, your account and, if you subscribe, Plus: performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • To keep the site secure and fair (limiting password guessing and new accounts, the page-leaving count, backups): our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • To keep records the law requires, for example about payments: legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

4. Cookies and storage in your browser

We set one cookie, bp_session, which keeps you logged in until you log out or have stayed away for two weeks; the cookie itself lasts a year at most. Your browser also stores a few settings locally, such as light or dark mode, the language of these pages and a game in progress. Both are necessary for the site to work, so there is no cookie banner. Nothing is used to follow you across websites.

5. Who else receives data

  • Your friends (players you have added and who have accepted) see your username, your picture and your results. Players in the same party lobby see your username and your guesses there.
  • Hosting: the site runs on a server rented from , which processes the data on our behalf.
  • Paddle: Plus is sold by Paddle.com Market Limited (United Kingdom) and its group companies as reseller and merchant of record. Paddle's payment form is only loaded when you click "Get Plus". Paddle then collects your payment details, email address and country as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We pass Paddle your username so the payment can be matched to your account. For transfers to the United Kingdom there is an adequacy decision of the European Commission.

The fonts and all other files of the site are served from our own server. No other third parties are contacted when you use Ballpark.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and game data: until you delete your account. That removes your account, picture, results and statistics from the live database at once.
  • Backups: the server makes a daily copy of the database and keeps the last 30. Deleted data disappears from the backups within that time.
  • Subscription records: as long as tax and commercial law require.

7. Your rights

You have the right to be told what we store about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to have its use restricted, to receive it in a common format, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Most of this you can do yourself: under "Account" (your name at the top of the site) you can remove your picture, change your password and delete your account. For a copy of everything stored about you, or anything else, write to ; we answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.

8. Children

Ballpark is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly store data about them.

9. Changes

If the site changes in a way that affects your data, we update this page and the date at the top.
