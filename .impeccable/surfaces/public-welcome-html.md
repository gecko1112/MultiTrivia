# Landing page (/welcome)

Mode: Persuade. Scope: the public page a visitor without an account sees.

Audience and job: invited friends and strangers alike, deciding whether Ballpark is for them. They read first; signing up comes at the end. Action: create an account. Proof on hand: real questions from the set, the five games themselves. No player numbers, quotes or ratings exist and none are shown. Constraints: little text, no hype, must look like the game pages. Example results are labelled as examples; prices are not stated here.

Memorable moment: a guess being judged, "1.13× too low", as the largest thing on the page.

Unresolved: whether this page replaces the login box at "/" (for now it lives at /welcome).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a reveal screen: one real question, a guess, and how close it was, at full scale. It refuses the headline, subcopy and button over a row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: The game pages' system. Indigo-slate ground, dark top bar with the wordmark, Barlow Condensed uppercase display, Inter text. Each game speaks in its own colour: red, tangerine, canary, verdigris, lavender. A number line with ticks and two markers. Verdicts in very large condensed caps.

STORY: The visitor sees a guess judged and understands that close counts. Scrolling, each game states itself as one worked example in its own colour field. Then: results are shared with friends only, playing is free, Plus is optional. They create an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar. Question at left, about 40px; the owner's line at right, about 44px. The verdict across the full width at roughly a sixth of the viewport width. Under it a log number line with the guess above and the answer below, the gap between them filled red. At the bottom edge a band with the other four games' example results and the "Your turn" button at its right end.

FORM: Guess and Verdict, position 5 on the ordered list, seed:f1fbfd232f8d14b5. Signature interaction, the reveal: the answer marker travels along the line from the guess to its place while the multiplier counts up; "Another question" replays it with a different real question. Motion grammar: things move along the scale with an exponential ease-out; nothing fades in from nowhere.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
